Privacy policy

This policy covers the Store Email Lab YouTube uploader and its public information pages.

Google and YouTube data

The uploader requests permission to read channel and video information and upload videos. It uses channel identifiers and upload records to check the destination, avoid duplicate uploads, and track the result. Google receives the media files and publishing details sent through its YouTube API.

Access and storage

When offline access is granted, a refresh token allows the uploader to reconnect without asking the channel owner to sign in for every upload. Credentials belong in protected storage. The uploader uses Google user data to operate the YouTube publishing workflow and does not sell it or use it for advertising. Store Email Lab retains upload records to track publishing and prevent duplicate uploads.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

The channel owner can revoke the uploader’s access from Google Account connections. For privacy questions or deletion of data held by the uploader, contact Store Email Lab using the support address shown on Google’s consent screen. Uploaded videos can be managed in YouTube Studio.

The uploader’s use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
